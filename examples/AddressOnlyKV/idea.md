## Name

AddressOnlyKV

## Title

No Distributed, Byte-Substantial Address-Only KV Class in Modern RoPE Decoders (With Sparse, Reproducible Early-Layer Exceptions): A Preregistered, Optimizer-Robust, Multi-Document Bounded Negative

## Short Hypothesis

Outside forced prefix/recent windows, modern instruction-tuned RoPE decoders (Qwen2.5-3B, Qwen2.5-7B, Mistral-7B) do NOT contain a distributed, byte-substantial aggregate class of non-prefix entries whose exact keys are useful while their values can be zeroed. The completed multi-document replay confirms this cleanly at the aggregate level: pooled 1.3415% ADDRESS and 0.6708% corrected byte-saving across 3 models x 3 PG-19 documents x endpoints {2K,4K,8K}, every model and document individually NO-GO versus the preregistered 20%/15% gates. The strong pointwise-absence claim remains false: 50 gate-passing strategy rows span 26/440 model/layer/head locations, with a reproducible Mistral layer-0 pocket (heads 1-4). The defensible claim is a BOUNDED NEGATIVE: no distributed reusable address-only cache primitive, but sparse reproducible early-layer exceptions. The remaining round closes the two framing-critical gaps the run exposed: (1) complete powered downstream equivalence for all three models -- especially Mistral, which hosts the exceptions; and (2) actually run the preregistered sink-overlap test to either justify or drop the 'sink-adjacent' characterization, and honestly scope the SubToken-min baseline.

## Related Work

The motivating softmax identity is unchanged: converting an entry from full KV to exact-key/zero-value removes exp(z_i)v_i while retaining exp(z_i) in the denominator Z, whereas dropping removes both and renormalizes. Adaptive-no-op attention-sink work and secondary-sink analyses give architecture-specific accounts of high-attention/low-message destinations; our exceptions are early-layer and (pending the sink-overlap test) possibly sink-adjacent. Gu et al.'s key-bias / non-informative-value account posits one origin of near-zero-message high-key-norm behavior; our data reject this as a universal, distributed explanation (median drop_err/address_err ~1.0 across 17.6M eligible entries). KV eviction methods (H2O, StreamingLLM, SnapKV, PyramidKV, Quest, VATP, ReST-KV) choose retain vs remove. Key/value asymmetry methods (VECTOR, Sub-Token Routing including its zero-value-group case, KVTuner, ShadowKV, Eigen Attention, AsymKV) establish keys and values can be treated differently. Our contribution is the empirical, preregistered, optimizer-robust, multi-document finding that under joint full/address/drop allocation with an exact reconstruction surrogate, a byte-substantial DISTRIBUTED non-prefix zero-message class does not exist in the tested modern RoPE decoders, that its rare reproducible instances are concentrated in early layers rather than forming a broadly reusable cache primitive, and that key/denominator and value importance are functionally inseparable outside these sparse pockets. This bounds optimistic readings of the adaptive-no-op and asymmetric-compression literatures.

## Abstract

Keys determine where attention can go; values determine what it retrieves. Can an inference cache profitably store some old, non-prefix keys with their values zeroed? We preregistered a strict conjunction test and built an optimizer-robust auditing framework (77 passing tests; exact ternary/binary enumeration; additive-surrogate allocation; multistart nonlinear local search that matched exhaustive optimization on all 15,840 audited strategy rows; independent reconstruction discrepancy ~5e-17). On fresh activations regenerated from three pinned RoPE decoders (Qwen2.5-3B, Qwen2.5-7B, Mistral-7B) over three disjoint PG-19 documents at query endpoints 2K/4K/8K, the aggregate result is a clean NEGATIVE: even the strongest allocator selects only 1.3415% ADDRESS with 0.6708% corrected byte saving, and every model and document is individually NO-GO against the preregistered 20% ADDRESS / 15% saving gates (per-model 1.12-1.73% ADDRESS; endpoint growth 1.07%->1.63% from 2K->8K but always far below gates). The pointwise 'absence everywhere' framing is falsified: 50 gate-passing strategy rows occur across 26 of 440 model/layer/head locations, with a reproducible Mistral layer-0 pocket (heads 1-4 passing across documents and endpoints). Matched-byte controls show median parity between AddressOnly, Binary, and ReST-min. Downstream RULER equivalence is established with power for Qwen2.5-3B (Address-Binary = -0.42 pts, power 0.928). This round completes the two framing-critical gaps the run exposed: (i) powered +/-1-point downstream equivalence for all three models, especially Mistral-7B which hosts the exceptions, and (ii) the preregistered sink-overlap test that either justifies or removes the 'sink-adjacent' label for the sparse pockets, plus an honest scoping of the SubToken-min baseline. We report a bounded, honest negative: no distributed byte-substantial address-only cache primitive across three RoPE decoders at contexts <=8K, with sparse reproducible early-layer exceptions, and value/denominator importance functionally inseparable outside them.

## Risk Factors And Limitations

- The finding is a bounded negative, not a strong absence: 50 gate-passing rows over 26/440 locations exist, including a reproducible Mistral layer-0 pocket. The paper must frame the claim as 'no DISTRIBUTED byte-substantial address-only class, with sparse reproducible exceptions' and never as pointwise absence.
- The 'sink-adjacent' characterization is currently unverified (the run only found modest early-layer enrichment, 30.77% vs 25%, driven by Mistral, and did not test conventional/secondary-sink overlap). The revised title/abstract keep the label ONLY if the step-3 sink-overlap test supports it; otherwise it is dropped in favor of 'sparse early-layer'.
- Downstream evidence is fully powered only for Qwen2.5-3B; Qwen2.5-7B and Mistral-7B require completion this round. Mistral is the model hosting the exceptions, so its powered equivalence is essential; if power targets cannot be met within budget, achieved sample and power are reported rather than asserting equivalence.
- Only three documents were completed (not the proposed 6-8), so document-cluster uncertainty is limited; however per-document aggregates are stable (1.29%/1.35%/1.38% ADDRESS), which supports the aggregate negative.
- The additive singleton surrogate is not an exact optimizer; the negative relies on validated nonlinear local search (matched exhaustive on all 15,840 rows) and exact enumeration, which must continue to hold.
- Only three models (Qwen2.5-3B/7B, Mistral-7B), two families, are tested; Llama-3.1-8B is gated. Generalization is narrowed to standard RoPE decoders and excludes ALiBi/NoPE, MoE, and >8B models.
- Contexts are <=8K; 16K, streaming, chat, and long-reasoning regimes are out of scope.
- The SubToken-min contextualization is limited to a restricted implementation and is reported only as implementation-specific context, not a general asymmetric-compression comparison.
- The negative is established under a specific exact-reconstruction surrogate and allocator family; exhaustive audits and validated local search substantially bound but cannot fully exclude that some other objective finds additional non-early-layer pockets.
