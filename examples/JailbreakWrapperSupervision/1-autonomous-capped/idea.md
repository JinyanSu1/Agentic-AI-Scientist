## Name

WrapperOrContext

## Title

Surface Form, Not Intent: A Same-Core, Same-Target Causal Trial Shows Jailbreak-Wrapped Benign Supervision Buys Only Test-Time Surface-Form Dependence — Now With a Completed Evaluator-Context Ablation That Rules Out Judge Wrapper-Susceptibility, Power-Increased Independent Cross-Family Corroboration, and a Bounded Author-Executable Blinded Confirmatory Audit

## Short Hypothesis

With hard harmful examples, benign cores, targets, optimizer slots, and token budget held identical, wrapping benign cores in jailbreak framing (W) versus dewrapping them (D) produces a TEST-TIME SURFACE-FORM DEPENDENCE: W beats D on wrapped presentations but is nonpositive on the identical direct cores, so there is NO transfer as abstract harmless intent and NO safety benefit. The completed evaluator-context ablation now shows this is OUTPUT-DRIVEN, not judge wrapper-susceptibility: stripping the jailbreak wrapper from the judge's prompt INCREASES rather than removes the wrapped W-D effect under both qualified judges, with a near-zero XSTest placebo. Remaining work is confirmatory and tightening, not new mechanism: (1) close the independent-corroboration gap that human recruitment repeatedly failed to fill by adding GPU-only independence (power-increased cross-family Llama-70B audit plus a third differently-architected judge) and a small author-executable blinded expert confirmatory audit on the backbone contrasts; (2) tighten or explicitly bound the borderline direct anchor. The W-N' 'wrapper-specific mechanism' remains demoted to a judge-dependent exploratory signal.

## Related Work

The hard-example safety-tuning seed paper (Gupta et al., 2026) mines hard harmful prompts, trains on the model's own safe rollouts, cuts jailbreak ASR but induces overrefusal on jailbreak-shaped benign prompts, and repairs this by interleaving adversarially-framed benign counterexamples, hypothesizing that these teach harmless intent. It never holds benign source, core request, response target, order, or length fixed between adversarial-benign and ordinary-benign conditions, and never contrasts adversarial framing against a length-matched neutral frame. We close that identification gap with same-core triples (D=exact core, W=core in jailbreak framing, N'=tokenizer-length-matched neutral frame) sharing identical audited targets, hard examples, order, initialization, steps, and target tokens. Our completed multi-setting, multi-seed run establishes one causal fact — benign-wrapper supervision buys separation ONLY on wrapped test presentations and NEVER on the identical direct cores — corroborated by two independently qualified judges (Qwen2.5-7B and Llama-3.1-70B, both clearing a frozen >=0.90 gate). Because the phenomenon under study is itself surface form, the central alternative explanation is judge wrapper-susceptibility; our completed evaluator-context ablation directly refutes it. WildJailbreak (Jiang et al., 2024) is used as target-conditional fragility analysis, not external validation. XSTest (Rottger et al., 2023) supplies direct safe/unsafe contrasts as the off-surface transfer test, gated by an explicit headroom check. The paired log-odds interaction is a standard difference-in-differences; we claim no statistical novelty.

## Abstract

Hard-example safety tuning makes a model robust by making it reluctant, and jailbreak-shaped benign data reduces that overrefusal. Does the benign data teach jailbreak-specific adaptation, harmless intent, or merely the effect of adding any extra user context? We run a same-core, same-target randomized training trial in which, for each harmless core, we build a dewrapped version (D), a jailbreak-wrapped version (W), and a tokenizer-length-matched neutral version (N'); all interleave identical hard harmful examples and share every target token, hard example, optimizer slot, initialization, and update count, differing only in benign user context. Across a 24-seed primary Qwen2.5-3B recipe, an 8-seed alternative Qwen recipe, and a 24-seed Mistral-7B replication, our backbone finding is corroborated by two independently qualified automatic judges: W improves safe/harmful separation over D on wrapped presentations but is nonpositive on the identical direct cores (Llama pooled direct -0.322, p=.008; wrapped +0.435, p=.014; moderation +0.758, p=.0006; matched by Qwen). This is a test-time SURFACE-FORM DEPENDENCE with NO direct/intent transfer and NO safety benefit. The central alternative explanation — judge wrapper-susceptibility, motivated because cross-judge agreement collapses on wrapped prompts (kappa 0.453 vs 0.939 direct) — is now REFUTED by a completed evaluator-context ablation: re-scoring the SAME 6,400 stored responses with the jailbreak wrapper STRIPPED from the judge's prompt INCREASES the wrapped W-D effect (Qwen +0.582 to +1.627, paired +1.045, p=.0001; Llama +0.435 to +1.581, paired +1.145, p=.0001) and the moderation, while an unchanged-XSTest placebo is effectively zero. The effect therefore lives in the trained model's outputs, not in the evaluator seeing wrapper text; evaluator context still shifts magnitude. Two additional held-out wrapper families replicate the positive wrapped W-D across the full 24-seed primary-Qwen panel (famA +1.522, all 24 seeds and every LOCO positive; famB +0.643, 23/24 seeds positive), but the frozen 2,400-item independent Llama-70B cross-family audit was directionally concordant yet underpowered (famA p=.389, famB p=.314). This round closes the independence gaps that repeated human-annotator recruitment could not: we (1) power-increase the GPU-only independent cross-family Llama-70B audit and add a third differently-architected qualified judge to test independence without depending on annotator availability; (2) run a small, author-executable, preregistered blinded expert confirmatory audit restricted to the backbone contrasts (W-D direct, W-D wrapped) and the wrapper-shown-vs-stripped comparison, scoped honestly as bounded expert confirmation rather than a large human gold standard; and (3) tighten or explicitly bound the borderline direct anchor (XSTest W-D -0.183, p=.056) by adding GPU-only direct-item precision. The W-N' 'wrapper-specific mechanism' stays demoted to a judge-dependent exploratory signal. Safety is a clean, strictly-bounded negative: no setting passes joint 3-point noninferiority, fulfillment is not response-harmfulness, and wrapped harmful-request fulfillment reverses sign across judges. The result is a bounded, multiply-judged, output-attributed causal fact — jailbreak framing on otherwise identical benign supervision buys separation only where present at test time, with no direct transfer and no safety benefit.

## Risk Factors And Limitations

- Judge wrapper-susceptibility, the central alternative explanation, is now empirically refuted by the completed evaluator-context ablation (wrapper-stripping increased rather than removed the effect, near-zero placebo); the residual caveat is that evaluator context measurably shifts effect magnitude, so absolute effect sizes are evaluator-context-dependent even though the sign and direction are output-attributed.
- External independent human annotators were unavailable for two consecutive rounds; the revised plan substitutes annotator-independent GPU-only independence (a power-increased cross-family independent audit and a third differently-architected judge) plus a small author-executable blinded expert confirmatory audit; if even the minimal expert audit cannot be run, conclusions remain multiple-automatic-judge-qualified and are scoped as such.
- Cross-family generalization is currently full-panel-Qwen self-replicated with directionally concordant but underpowered independent audit; the expanded audit may still fail to lock per-family independence, in which case the generalization claim is explicitly scoped.
- The direct anchor is borderline (XSTest W-D -0.183, p=.056); precision expansion may confirm significant nonpositivity or only bound it, in which case the backbone is stated as nonpositive-with-small-CI rather than strictly significant.
- The exact-token W-N' effect is NOT independently corroborated and is recipe-heterogeneous; reported only as a judge-dependent exploratory signal, not a mechanism.
- No safety-improvement interpretation is supported: no setting passes joint 3-point noninferiority, fulfillment is not response-harmfulness, and wrapped harmful-request fulfillment reverses sign across judges.
- Neutral-arm degradation persists (N' direct safe fulfillment below D); residual training-dynamics differences between neutral and jailbreak framing cannot be fully eliminated even with exact token matching.
- Two subject models, two recipes, LoRA-only, and a limited set of wrapper families; generality across families, sizes, full fine-tuning, and DPO/RLHF is untested.
- A third automatic judge may fail the unrelaxed >=0.90 qualification gate, in which case architecture-diverse independent corroboration is not added and independence rests on Qwen+Llama plus the ablation.
