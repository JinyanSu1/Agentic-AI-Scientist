## Name

IncrementalWrapperAdvantage

## Title

Benign Supervision Transfers, But Its Jailbreak-Wrapper Advantage Is Presentation-Dependent: A Same-Core, Same-Target Causal Trial Anchored to a Normalization-Matched Harmful-Only Control and Re-Judged Across Two Model Families

## Short Hypothesis

With hard harmful examples, benign cores, targets, optimizer slots, and token budget held identical, benign supervision (both dewrapped D and jailbreak-wrapped W) substantially improves direct-core fulfillment separation relative to a normalization-matched harmful-only control (H_norm) -- so there IS real transfer. The surviving, narrower causal claim is that the INCREMENTAL advantage of W over D is presentation-dependent: nonpositive on direct core presentations and positive on wrapped presentations. This must hold across three settings and both cross-family automatic judges before locking.

## Related Work

The hard-example safety-tuning seed paper (Gupta et al., 2026) mines hard harmful prompts, trains on the model's own safe rollouts, cuts jailbreak ASR but induces overrefusal on jailbreak-shaped benign prompts, and repairs this by interleaving adversarially-framed benign counterexamples, hypothesizing that these teach harmless intent. It never holds benign source, core request, response target, order, or length fixed, and never contrasts adversarial framing against a length-matched neutral frame OR against a harmful-only reference. Our earlier draft over-claimed a 'no transfer / surface form not intent' conclusion from a W-minus-D contrast alone; the reviewer-flagged confound was that D also receives benign supervision, so W-D is silent on absolute transfer. We close this by adding a normalization-matched harmful-only control H_norm (identical hard examples, optimizer slots, steps, initialization, and total supervised-token denominator via virtual normalization tokens, but NO benign text, labels, token IDs, or gradients) plus a slot-placebo H_slotplacebo. The result decisively refutes 'no transfer': D-H_norm and W-H_norm are large and positive on direct cores. What survives is a bounded, defensible fact: the incremental W-over-D advantage is presentation-dependent, coexisting with substantial benign-supervision transfer. WildJailbreak (Jiang et al., 2024) is a target-conditional fragility analysis; XSTest (Rottger et al., 2023) supplies direct safe/unsafe contrasts. The paired log-odds interaction is a standard difference-in-differences; we claim no statistical novelty.

## Abstract

Hard-example safety tuning makes a model robust by making it reluctant, and jailbreak-shaped benign data reduces that overrefusal. Does that benign data teach jailbreak-specific surface adaptation, transferable harmless behavior, or merely the effect of adding any extra user context? Our earlier draft claimed benign-wrapper supervision buys only test-time surface-form dependence with no transfer -- but that conclusion rested on a wrapped-minus-dewrapped (W-D) contrast in which BOTH arms already receive benign supervision, so it could never establish absence of transfer. This round adds the reviewer-flagged missing control: a normalization-matched harmful-only arm H_norm (identical hard harmful examples, optimizer slots, updates, initialization, and total supervised-token denominator via virtual normalization tokens, but no benign text, labels, token IDs, or gradients), plus a slot-placebo H_slotplacebo. In a same-core, same-target randomized training trial (dewrapped D, jailbreak-wrapped W, tokenizer-length-matched neutral N', harmful-only H_norm) sharing every target token, hard example, optimizer slot, initialization, and update count, we find that benign supervision DOES transfer: across 24 primary-Qwen seeds, D-H_norm on direct/XSTest presentations is +2.40 log-odds (95% CI [+2.12,+3.25], p=.0002) and W-H_norm is +2.21 [+1.99,+2.96], p=.0002; direct safe/harmful fulfillment rates are D 0.81/0.07, W 0.75/0.05, H_norm 0.22/0.001. H_norm and H_slotplacebo are indistinguishable, so the effect is not a loss-denominator artifact. We therefore RETRACT the 'no transfer / learned surface form rather than intent' claim. The surviving, calibrated causal fact is narrower and robust: the INCREMENTAL advantage of W over D is presentation-dependent -- nonpositive on direct core presentations and positive on wrapped presentations -- while coexisting with substantial transfer from benign supervision, and it does not identify a latent intent mechanism. Because judge-dependence is a live threat exactly on wrapped prompts (cross-judge fulfillment agreement collapses to 0.788, kappa 0.453 wrapped vs 0.971, kappa 0.939 direct), we re-score the newly judged H_norm/H_slotplacebo responses with the qualified cross-family Llama-3.1-70B judge and report D-H_norm, W-H_norm, and W-D presentation-dependence per judge and per setting with preserved seed/item clustering. We also report the evaluator-context (wrapper-stripped) ablation explicitly, stating whether the wrapped moderation survives when the judge cannot see the wrapper. Safety remains a clean, strictly-bounded negative: fulfillment is not response-harmfulness, no setting passes joint 3-point noninferiority, and no safety-improvement claim is made. The result is a bounded, doubly-judged causal fact -- benign supervision transfers substantially, and only its jailbreak-wrapper advantage is presentation-dependent -- with the original strong hypothesis honestly refuted.

## Risk Factors And Limitations

- The original strong hypothesis ('no transfer / surface form not intent') is refuted by H_norm: both D and W substantially improve direct-core fulfillment relative to harmful-only tuning. The paper is reframed around the narrower surviving claim and must not retain any 'no transfer' language.
- The pivotal H_norm transfer result was scored by the Qwen judge only until this round; the study's own cross-family terminal standard requires Llama-70B re-scoring, which is the one non-cuttable new deliverable. If transfer does not reproduce under Llama-70B, it must be scoped single-judge-qualified.
- The experiment demonstrates behavioral transfer relative to harmful-only tuning but does NOT identify latent 'intent' as a mechanism; surface-form sensitivity and intent learning are not mutually exclusive and are not disentangled here.
- Evaluator wrapper-susceptibility remains a live threat because cross-judge agreement collapses on wrapped prompts (0.788, kappa 0.453); the incremental-advantage claim is trained-behavior-attributable only if the wrapped moderation survives wrapper-stripped judging, which must be reported explicitly.
- Human corroboration was prepared but not executed (no annotators available); conclusions are two-automatic-judge-qualified. This is the intended, honestly-stated terminal state, not a reason to defer completion.
- No safety-improvement interpretation is supported: no joint 3-point noninferiority passes, fulfillment is not response-harmfulness, and wrapped harmful-request fulfillment direction reverses across judges.
- Neutral-arm degradation persists (N' direct safe fulfillment below D); residual training-dynamics differences cannot be fully eliminated even with token matching.
- Only one held-out wrapped source family (FlowGPT), two subject models, LoRA-only, two recipes; generality across families, sizes, full fine-tuning, and DPO/RLHF is untested.
- Only two settings for heterogeneity means recipe-heterogeneity evidence is not population-level; evaluation-item variance dominates and primary-recipe borderline results cannot be resolved by adding training seeds alone.
